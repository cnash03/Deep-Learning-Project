This final milestone represents the culmination of your team’s work — demonstrating your ability to plan, implement, and communicate a complete deep learning project. You’ll deliver both a technical paper and a presentation summarizing your results, insights, and lessons learned. This submission should reflect a polished, professional-level project suitable for sharing with peers and stakeholders, similar to an internal research showcase or industry demo.

Deliverables
Elect one group member to submit the following:

Final Project Paper
A formal writeup that includes:

Abstract: 100–150 words summarizing your problem, method, and findings

Introduction & Motivation: Why the problem matters and what you aimed to achieve

Related Work: Summarize 1–2 relevant references or benchmarks

Methods: Model architecture(s), training setup, preprocessing steps

Experiments & Results: Metrics, validation strategy, key findings

Error Analysis: Major takeaways from misclassifications or model behavior

Discussion & Future Work: What worked, what didn’t, and possible extensions

Ethical Considerations: Bias, fairness, or privacy reflections

References (APA or IEEE format)

 Final Presentation (6-8 minutes, video)
A recorded group presentation that communicates the essence of your work.

Explain the problem, data, and approach

Visualize your model or workflow (architecture diagram encouraged)

Present your results and highlight major findings

Reflect on lessons learned and next steps

Each group member must contribute (visibly or vocally)

 Peer Showcase Participation
 Each group will share their project video and respond to at least two questions or comments from classmates in the course forum/discussion space.

Updated Risk & Ethics Reflection 
Include a short section or slide summarizing what ethical or data integrity issues emerged and how they were managed.

Final Figure or Visualization
Include at least one clear figure (chart, confusion matrix, or visualization) that captures your key result or insight.

Grading
Your group will be graded as such:

Technical Soundness & Implementation: 30%
Experimental Rigor & Analysis: 25%
Clarity & Structure (Paper + Video): 20%
Reproducibility & Documentation: 15%
Insight & Reflection: 10%
200 points possible. See the rubric for exact grading criteria.
