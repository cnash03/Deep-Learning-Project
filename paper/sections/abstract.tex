\begin{abstract}
Off-road robots must tell drivable dirt and gravel from water, logs, and dense
vegetation, yet off-road scenes lack the structure that urban driving models
rely on, and the classes that matter most for safety are among the rarest. We
study pixel-wise terrain segmentation on the RUGD dataset with two
encoder--decoder networks, U-Net and DeepLabV3+, each built on an
ImageNet-pretrained ResNet encoder, and compare four imbalance-aware training
losses against plain cross-entropy under a fixed sequence-level split and
validation-only model selection. Our best model,
